Baseball Scorekeeper | iOS 3.1.4 (build 1) | Simplified Chinese UI. No app account or password is required. Use fictional names. Both manual scoring modes (基础版 and Pro 版) are included.

One device: Open 我的 > 练习一局, select Pro 版 and start practice. Tap 击球进入场内 > 滚地出局, choose a ground ball and a location, then tap the third baseman (5) and the first baseman (3) in order. The route shows 5 → 3. Practice is separate from official statistics.

Two devices, online: Use two independent installations of 3.1.4, with different team member identities. On A, open 球队 > 球队共享 and share a local team with a fictional roster. If starting from an empty team, add enough fictional players for a starting lineup. Tap 邀请成员. On B, choose 通过邀请加入 and join via A’s link. A refreshes the team and uses 成员管理 to change B from 成员 (member) to 记录员 (scorer) and confirms. Do not pair B as another device of the same member.

On A as team administrator, open 比赛 > 开始新比赛, select the shared team, enter a fictional opponent and turn on 安排未来比赛. Enable 现在完成赛前设置 and 确认阵容后发布到球队赛程. Continue through setup and confirm the lineups to publish. Open that game from 比赛 > 即将进行. In 本场记录岗位 > 安排记录员, assign A to 整场或局面记录员 and B to 阵容记录员（可选）. If prompted, provide both starting lineups from the creating device. With no assignment, only the administrator who created the game can score. With one assignment, that person handles the entire game.

On B, open the same game, select 在此设备管理阵容, check both lineups and tap 提交双方先发. On A, select 核对阵容并进入记分 and confirm the first batter and starting pitcher. Record a ball. On B, open 管理阵容 for either side. In the bench section tap 添加本场队员. Select an omitted roster player or enter a temporary player with jersey number 00. Add to the bench and save. Keep the scoring screen open and online on A. Once applied, the player is available as a substitute. Adding a player does not itself make a substitution. The attached illustrated guide shows the Chinese controls.

Optional voice scoring (separate from the features above): reviewer activation code [INSERT UNUSED PRIVATE CODE]. Redeem in 我的 > App 设置 > 语音记分. In a regular game tap the microphone, accept the disclosure and microphone permission, say “坏球”, then check the transcript, analyze and confirm. For optional game analysis, open a completed official game and select 比赛分析 > 生成比赛分析 after reading the disclosure.
