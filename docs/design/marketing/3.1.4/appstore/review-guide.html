棒球比赛记录大师 · Baseball ScorekeeperApp Review　/　iOS 3.1.4 (1)

Score one game on two devices

Use two independent team members on the same shared team. On device A, publish a scheduled game with both starting lineups.
Invite B, then change B’s team role to 记录员 (scorer) in 成员管理. Both devices must be online and open the same game.

DEVICE A · ADMINISTRATOR

Assign the two scorers

Open 本场记录岗位 → 安排记录员. Choose A in row one and B in row two. Tap 保存.

DEVICE B → DEVICE A

Confirm the starters

B taps 在此设备管理阵容 and 提交双方先发. A checks the first batter and pitcher, then taps 确认并开始.

DEVICE B · LINEUP SCORER

Add a late arrival

Open 管理阵容 → 添加本场队员. Choose a roster player or register a temporary one, then add to the bench and save.

Expected result　·　With A’s scoring screen open, the new player becomes available on the bench. Record a substitution to put that player into the game.
