App: 棒球比赛记录大师 (BaseballMaster)
Version: 2.3 (Build 1)
Bundle ID: com.jasonchen.baseballmaster

No login or review account is required. No subscriptions, in-app purchases, ads or third-party analytics. The UI is Simplified Chinese. Core scoring, editable sample teams, corrections, statistics, reports and local backups work offline.

This update adds configurable adult slow-pitch scoring and improves pregame lineups and pitcher-outs accounting. It records the scorer's confirmed decisions; it does not certify eligibility or automatically decide tournament-specific rules.

Review steps:
1. In 比赛 (Games), create a 本队比赛 (Team Game). Choose 成人慢垒 (Adult Slow Pitch), 10 fielders, a positive duration (e.g. 90 minutes), initial counts (e.g. 1 strike/1 ball) and one of the explicit two-strike foul choices. Editable fictional teams are available. Add sample players if a selected team has fewer than 10 available players.
2. Continue to both teams' lineups. Assign all 10 positions including 自由人 (Free); additional batting-only players can be added. Confirm creation. Scoring opens before Play Ball: 阵容 (Lineup) still lets you set both starting lineups. Play Ball opens a confirmation; confirming starts the clock. Pregame adjustments are initial setup and are excluded from exported plate-appearance details. Live lineup changes remain in the game records.
3. Record pitches. The initial count is not a physical pitch. With 1/1 initial counts, three actual balls produce a walk and two actual strikes produce a strikeout. Under 再允许一次界外, the first foul after two strikes uses the extra chance and the next ends the appearance. Illegal offers the umpire's final ball, swinging/foul, batted-ball or No Pitch result and whether a pitch was physically thrown.
4. Use 阵容 to adjust either side after starting. Extra hitters can enter defense while retaining their batting order; the field keeps 10 players. Time expiry prompts the scorer rather than automatically truncating a plate appearance.
5. In results, open Box Score, detailed or text-only PDF/TXT reports; select individual appearances. Historical corrections use a draft and impact preview before saving. Pitcher outs are counted once, including runner outs; three actual outs equal 1.0 innings, while a manually ended two-out half remains 0.2.
6. To review TEXT live sharing, open the antenna icon during a temporary fictional match, enable 文字直播 and confirm the public-data notice. Open the generated HTTPS link or scan its QR code in Safari. The viewer is read-only and shows scores, rules, current lineups and events. The app needs internet only for this optional live service. Close the test broadcast afterwards.

Live service: https://baseballmaster.cc/livestreaming/novideo/
Temporary links are generated by the reviewer's own test match; no permanent replay/account is provided. Anyone with a link can view. Successful closing deletes public content; finished or unsynced matches expire after one hour. Local phone records remain. There is no audio/video live streaming, background-delivery guarantee or cloud backup of the full local database.
